How This Is Calculated

  • Deposits: Security deposit commonly matches the tenancy tenure in months (e.g. 2 months for a 2-year tenancy), plus a half-month utility deposit and any optional renovation/other deposits.
  • Tenancy Stamping Fee: RM1 per RM250 (or part thereof) of annual rental for tenancies of 1 year or less, RM3 per RM250 for tenancies longer than 1 year, minimum RM10.
  • Agency Fee: Optional, entered in months of rental, with 8% SST added on top if applicable.
  • Booking Fee SST: An optional 8% SST can be added on the first month's booking payment.
  • Disclaimer: This tool is a guide only. Actual amounts payable depend on what's agreed between landlord, tenant, and agent in the tenancy agreement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What costs are included in a tenant rental quote?

1 month advance rental, a security deposit, a utility deposit, the tenancy agreement stamping fee, and an agency fee if applicable.

How is the tenancy agreement stamping fee calculated?

RM1 per RM250 of annual rent (tenancies of 1 year or less) or RM3 per RM250 (longer than 1 year), minimum RM10.

Is this quote generator only for landlords?

It's built for landlords and agents to generate a move-in cost breakdown to send tenants via WhatsApp, but tenants can use it too to double-check what they're being asked to pay.
