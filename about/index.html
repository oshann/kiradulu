About KiraDulu

Kira dulu, baru decide — calculate first, then decide.

KiraDulu is a set of free calculators for everyday Malaysian money decisions — what you actually take home, how much tax you owe, whether a property pays for itself, and what a car really costs each month.

Every calculator uses Malaysian rules: LHDN tax brackets, KWSP and PERKESO contribution tables, JPJ road tax and current stamp duty rates, updated for 2025/2026.
